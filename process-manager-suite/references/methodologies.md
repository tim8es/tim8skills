# Methodologies and Frameworks

## BPMN 2.0 (Business Process Model and Notation)

A standard for visualizing business processes. Used to create AS-IS and TO-BE diagrams.

**Main elements:**
- **Events** — start, end, intermediate events
- **Tasks** — atomic actions
- **Gateways** — branching conditions (XOR, AND, OR)
- **Pools & Lanes** — division of responsibility between participants
- **Flows** — execution sequence

**When to use:**
- Documenting the current process (AS-IS)
- Designing the optimized process (TO-BE)
- Communicating with the team and stakeholders

**Tools:** Miro, Lucidchart, draw.io, Visio

---

## SIPOC (Suppliers, Inputs, Process, Outputs, Customers)

A high-level description of a process for quickly understanding its boundaries and participants.

**Structure:**
- **Suppliers** — who provides the inputs?
- **Inputs** — what data/resources are needed?
- **Process** — main steps (2-5 steps)
- **Outputs** — what does the process produce?
- **Customers** — who receives the result?

**When to use:**
- Initial process analysis
- Defining process boundaries
- Quick alignment with stakeholders

---

## Lean/Six Sigma

A methodology for identifying and eliminating waste (Muda) in processes.

**Seven types of waste (Muda):**
1. Overproduction
2. Waiting
3. Transportation
4. Over-processing
5. Inventory (excess stock)
6. Motion (unnecessary movement)
7. Defects

**Tools:**
- **5S** — organizing the workspace (Sort, Set, Shine, Standardize, Sustain)
- **Kaizen** — continuous improvement
- **Value Stream Mapping** — visualizing the value stream

**When to use:**
- Analyzing process inefficiency
- Finding opportunities for optimization
- Calculating potential savings

---

## Value Stream Mapping (VSM)

Visualization of all process steps, highlighting value-adding actions and waste.

**VSM structure:**
- Timeline
- Value-add activities
- Non-value-add activities
- Cycle time
- Lead time

**When to use:**
- Detailed process analysis
- Identifying bottlenecks
- Calculating efficiency metrics

---

## Choosing a Methodology

| Situation | Methodology | Why |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Initial process analysis | SIPOC | Fast, high-level |
| Process documentation | BPMN 2.0 | Standard, understood by everyone |
| Finding waste and optimization | Lean/VSM | Focus on efficiency |
| Detailed flow analysis | Value Stream Mapping | All time-based metrics are visible |
